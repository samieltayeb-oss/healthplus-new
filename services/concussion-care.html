Evidence-Based Concussion Management Program

HealthPlus is proud to offer the Complete Concussions Program at our clinic. As a certified location, we provide a full evidence-based concussion management program—helping our patients and athletes safely return to learn, work, and play.

Concussion Recovery & Treatment Strategy

Following a concussion injury, it is recommended that you seek the care of a healthcare professional with training and experience in concussion as soon as possible. The earlier you seek quality concussion care, the better your recovery will be.

Comprehensive Care Protocol

Generally, a period of relative "symptom-limited" physical and cognitive rest is recommended for the first 24-48 hours after injury. After this rest period, a gradual increase in mental and physical activity is recommended under the supervision of a licensed practitioner.

Multimodal Pre-season Baseline Testing

Pre-season testing measures your balance, reaction time, memory, and visual tracking. If you sustain a concussion during the season, we compare your recovery against your personal healthy baseline.

Alberta Health Care Insurance Plan (AHCIP) Coverage

Medically necessary physician consultations and diagnostic evaluations are fully covered under AHCIP. Optional baseline testing and third-party athletic reports will always be transparently discussed prior to assessment.

Overview & Care Focus

Your physician will perform a comprehensive neurological examination assessing your cognitive function, balance, vision tracking, coordination, and symptom profile. Using the Complete Concussions™ evidence-based framework, they will identify which physiological subtypes of concussion are affecting you and develop a stepwise recovery plan tailored to your timeline. Follow-up appointments monitor your symptom progression, adjust your activity levels appropriately, and ensure you do not return to high-risk activities before you are medically cleared.

Who This Is For

Concussion care is for anyone who has recently sustained a head injury — whether from sport, a motor vehicle accident, a fall, or a workplace incident — as well as those experiencing persistent or worsening post-concussion symptoms. Early, expert assessment significantly improves recovery outcomes. Athletes requiring pre-season baseline testing and formal return-to-sport clearance also benefit from our program.

Preparation & What To Bring

If you experienced a loss of consciousness, repeated vomiting, seizures, extreme confusion, or severe worsening headaches after the injury, seek emergency care immediately. For clinic visits, bring a trusted friend or family member if you are experiencing memory difficulties. Bring details of the injury (date, mechanism, and any initial symptoms), and a list of any medications you are currently taking. Avoid screen time and excessive cognitive activity in the 24 hours before your appointment if symptoms are still acute.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a concussion take to heal? +
Recovery time varies significantly by individual and injury severity. Most concussions in healthy adults resolve within 7–14 days with appropriate management. Youth athletes and those with previous concussion history may take longer. Post-concussion syndrome — where symptoms persist beyond 4 weeks — affects approximately 15–30% of patients and requires a more targeted treatment approach.
Can you provide a doctor's note for school or work? +
Yes. We provide detailed physician documentation outlining your diagnosis, recommended cognitive and physical rest requirements, and a phased return-to-learn or return-to-work plan. These notes are tailored to what your school, employer, or sports organization requires.
What is the Complete Concussions™ program? +
Complete Concussions™ is a North American network of certified clinics offering an evidence-based, multimodal approach to concussion management. As a certified clinic, HealthPlus uses their validated assessment tools, treatment protocols, and the Concussion Tracker app to give patients the most current, proven care available.
Is concussion care covered by AHCIP? +
Medically necessary physician consultations and neurological assessments are fully covered under AHCIP. Pre-season baseline testing and third-party athletic clearance reports may involve additional fees, which are always discussed with you transparently before any charges are incurred.

Conditions & Focus

  • Recent concussions and mild traumatic brain injuries (mTBI)
  • Post-concussion syndrome (headaches, dizziness, brain fog)
  • Sports-related head impacts and collision injuries
  • Balance, vision, and vestibular disturbances post-concussion
  • Cognitive and memory difficulties after head injury
  • Medical clearance for return to play, school, or work
