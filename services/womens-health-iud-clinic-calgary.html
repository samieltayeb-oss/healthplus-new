Comprehensive Women's Health Services in South Calgary

At HealthPlus by SEHA Medical, our physicians provide respectful, individualized care across every stage of a woman's life. Whether you are scheduling routine cervical screening, exploring contraception options, planning a pregnancy, or seeking support for perimenopause and menopause, our clinical team offers clear medical guidance in a comfortable setting.

Preventive & Cervical Screening

Routine Pap tests, STI screening, breast health assessments, and age-appropriate preventive screening requisitions.

Contraception & IUD Consultations

Evidence-based counseling on hormonal and non-hormonal contraception, including IUD assessment, insertion, and removal planning.

Preconception & Prenatal Care

Fertility baseline workups, early prenatal consultations, and seamless coordination with our in-house Obstetrics & Gynecology specialist.

Perimenopause & Menopause Care

Assessment of hormonal changes, symptom management options, bone density screening, and long-term cardiovascular health.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral for a women's health or contraception consultation at HealthPlus Midnapore? +
Primary women's health consultations, Pap screenings, and contraception counseling can be booked directly with our Midnapore clinic at (403) 455-6656. Specialist Obstetrics & Gynecology consultations are coordinated by physician referral.
Are women's health visits covered by Alberta Health Care (AHCIP)? +
Yes. Medically necessary consultations, cervical screenings, and clinical procedures are covered with a valid Alberta Health Care Insurance Plan (AHCIP) card. Prescription devices (such as an IUD) are dispensed through a pharmacy and may be covered by your private or provincial drug plan.
Can I request a female physician for my women's health appointment? +
Yes. HealthPlus by SEHA Medical has experienced female physicians on our Midnapore medical team. Please let our reception team know your preference when booking at (403) 455-6656.
