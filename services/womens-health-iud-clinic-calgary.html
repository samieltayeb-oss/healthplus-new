Comprehensive Women's Health Services at Every Life Stage

At HealthPlus by SEHA Medical, our physicians provide respectful, trauma-informed, and individualized care across every stage of a woman's life. Whether you are scheduling routine cervical screening, exploring hormonal or non-hormonal contraception, planning a pregnancy, or seeking support for perimenopause and menopause, our clinical team offers clear guidance in a private setting.

Preventive & Cervical Screening: Routine Pap smears, HPV/STI screening, and breast health exams.
Contraception & IUD Clinic: Hormonal & copper IUD consultations, insertion, removal, and family planning.
Preconception & Prenatal Care: Early pregnancy monitoring and in-house OB/GYN specialist coordination.
Perimenopause & Menopause: Symptom evaluation, HRT discussion, bone density & cardiovascular wellness.

Patient Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral for a women's health or contraception consultation at HealthPlus Midnapore? +
Primary women's health consultations, Pap screenings, and contraception counseling can be booked directly with our Midnapore clinic at (403) 455-6656 or online. Specialist Obstetrics & Gynecology consultations are coordinated by physician referral.
Are women's health visits covered by Alberta Health Care (AHCIP)? +
Yes. Medically necessary consultations, cervical screenings, and clinical procedures are covered with a valid Alberta Health Care Insurance Plan (AHCIP) card. Prescription devices (such as an IUD) are dispensed through a pharmacy and may be covered by your private or provincial drug plan.
Can I request a female physician for my women's health appointment? +
Yes. HealthPlus by SEHA Medical has experienced female family physicians and a female Obstetrician-Gynecologist on our Midnapore team. Please let our reception team know your preference when booking at (403) 455-6656.
