Injury & Recovery · Clinical Overview

What to Expect at Your MVA Care Visit

Following a motor vehicle accident, a thorough medical evaluation is critical — even if you feel fine initially. Your physician will conduct a detailed physical examination focusing on your musculoskeletal and neurological systems, document all injuries accurately, order necessary imaging such as X-rays or MRI referrals, and initiate a structured treatment pathway. We also complete the physician sections of your Alberta insurance forms and WCB documentation, reducing administrative burden during your recovery.

Comprehensive medical evaluation to identify acute and delayed injuries.
Proper medical documentation required for insurance, WCB, and recovery claims.
Coordinated care plans including physiotherapy, imaging, and specialist referrals.
Scope of Care

Conditions & Clinical Needs We Address

Our Midnapore medical team evaluates, diagnoses, and coordinates care for:

Whiplash and cervical spine injuries
Soft tissue injuries and muscle strains
Post-collision headaches, dizziness, or concussion
Low back pain and joint injuries
Psychological impact of the accident (anxiety, PTSD)
Ongoing pain and reduced range of motion
Patient Eligibility

Who This Service Is For

MVA Care is for anyone who has been involved in a motor vehicle collision — as a driver, passenger, cyclist, or pedestrian — regardless of the severity of the accident or how you feel immediately after. Many injuries, including whiplash and concussion, may have delayed symptom onset. Seeking medical assessment within 24–72 hours of an accident is strongly recommended.

Before Your Visit

How to Prepare

Please bring the details of your accident (date, location, and a brief description), your insurance provider information and any claim forms that require physician completion, a list of your current medications, and your health card. If you have already been to an emergency department, bring your discharge summary.

Patient Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About MVA Care

Should I see a doctor even if I feel fine after an accident? +
Yes, absolutely. Soft tissue injuries like whiplash and concussion symptoms can take 24–72 hours — or longer — to fully present. Getting assessed early not only protects your health but also establishes an official medical record for your insurance claim.
Do you handle the insurance paperwork? +
Yes. Our physicians complete the required medical sections of Alberta automobile insurance forms, WCB documentation, and other third-party reports. We work directly with your insurer to ensure your records are accurate and timely.
Is MVA care covered by AHCIP? +
The medical assessment itself is covered by AHCIP. Depending on your insurer and policy, physiotherapy, imaging, and specialist visits may be covered through your auto insurance claim. Our team will help clarify what is covered in your specific situation.
Can I be referred to physiotherapy or other specialists? +
Yes. MVA recovery often requires a multidisciplinary approach. We coordinate referrals to physiotherapists, chiropractors, neurologists, and mental health professionals as part of your comprehensive recovery plan.
