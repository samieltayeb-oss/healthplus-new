Clear Patient Pathways

How to Book or Visit HealthPlus Midnapore

Whether you are looking for a permanent family doctor, need same-day walk-in attention, or require a specialist consultation, we make your next step clear.

01
Longitudinal Primary Care

New Family Practice Patients

Looking for a dedicated family physician in South Calgary? Several of our CCFP and MRCGP-certified family doctors are actively accepting new patients and families.

  • Schedule a structured Meet & Greet Appointment online or by phone.
  • Bring your valid Alberta Health Care (AHCIP) card and current medication list.
  • Seamless care for infants, children, adults, and seniors.
Request Meet & Greet Online →
02
Immediate Non-Emergency Care

Same-Day Walk-In Patients

Experiencing an unexpected illness, minor injury, infection, or urgent prescription renewal? Our walk-in clinic welcomes all patients—no prior registration required.

  • Walk in Monday–Saturday during clinic operating hours at 100-290 Midpark Way SE.
  • Call (403) 455-6656 to check current walk-in availability.
  • On-site lab requisitions, imaging referrals, and MVA/injury documentation.
Walk-In Clinic Details →
03
In-House Specialty Consultations

Referred Specialist Patients

Our Midnapore facility hosts specialist consultants in Obstetrics & Gynecology, Pediatrics, Internal Medicine & Infectious Diseases, Psychiatry, and Sport Medicine.

  • Request a referral from a HealthPlus family/walk-in doctor or your external physician.
  • Referring clinics can fax referrals directly to (403) 648-1876.
  • Our specialist coordinator contacts you directly to confirm your appointment.

Dr. Hatim Kheir is a Family Physician and Medical Director with clinical focus in emergency and urgent care, chronic disease management, women's health, sexual and reproductive healthcare, minor surgical procedures, and point-of-care ultrasound.

Trained in the United Kingdom, Dr. Kheir holds board certification in Family Medicine (MRCGP), the Diploma of the Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists (DRCOG), and the Diploma of the Faculty of Sexual and Reproductive Healthcare (DFSRH).
